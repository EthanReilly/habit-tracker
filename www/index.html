The method

To build a habit

  1. Make it obvious. Tie it to a time, a place or a habit you already do.
  2. Make it attractive. Pair it with something you look forward to.
  3. Make it easy. Shrink it to a two-minute version.
  4. Make it satisfying. Reward yourself right away.

To break a habit

  1. Make it invisible. Remove the cue from your surroundings.
  2. Make it unattractive. Spell out what it really costs you.
  3. Make it difficult. Put more steps between you and it.
  4. Make it unsatisfying. Find someone to hold you to account.
